Education and Experience

Rajesh K. Ahir holds a Ph.D. from the Indian Institute of Technology (IIT) Kharagpur and has over ten years of experience in research and university teaching across Singapore and India, spanning energy research institutes, an international research centre and engineering colleges.

Education

  • Ph.D. (2018 – 2023) – Indian Institute of Technology (IIT) Kharagpur, West Bengal, India.
    Thesis: Smart Meter Data Analytics: Aligning Data and Intelligence for Sustainable Energy Management. Advised by Prof. Basab Chakraborty and partially by Prof. Pabitra Mitra. About the thesis research.
  • M.Eng. (2010 – 2012) – Gujarat Technological University, Gujarat, India.
  • B.Eng. (2006 – 2010) – Veer Narmad South Gujarat University, Gujarat, India.

Work Experience

  • September 2026 – Present: Research Fellow, Energy Research Institute @ NTU (ERI@N) and College of Computing and Data Science (CCDS), Nanyang Technological University (NTU), Singapore.
  • June 2025: Visiting Researcher (3 weeks), G2Elab, Université Grenoble Alpes (UGA), Grenoble, France.
  • March 2024 – August 2026: Research Fellow, DesCartes project, CNRS@CREATE Ltd. Singapore, funded by the National Research Foundation (NRF) Singapore.
  • November 2023 – February 2024: Assistant Professor (Contractual), Indian Institute of Information Technology (IIIT) Surat, Gujarat, India.
  • August 2012 – June 2018: Assistant Professor, G. H. Patel College of Engineering & Technology (GCET), Vallabh Vidyanagar, Gujarat, India.
