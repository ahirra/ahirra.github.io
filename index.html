Hello (નમસ્તે), I'm Rajesh (રાજેશ)

Dr. Rajesh K. Ahir is a Research Fellow in the Energy Research Institute @ NTU (ERI@N) at Nanyang Technological University (NTU) Singapore, supervised by Prof. Nouredine Hadjsaid and Dr. Ramprakash Kathiresan. Before joining ERI@N, he was working with Prof. Benoit Delinchant and Prof. Arvind Easwaran in the DesCartes project at CNRS@CREATE Ltd. Singapore, funded by the National Research Foundation (NRF) Singapore (March 2024 – August 2026). He earned his Ph.D. from the Indian Institute of Technology (IIT) Kharagpur, West Bengal, India, advised by Prof. Basab Chakraborty and partially by Prof. Pabitra Mitra. His doctoral research focused on aligning data and artificial intelligence (AI) for sustainable energy management, using smart meter data analytics for smart grids (read about his research). He has been a Senior Member, IEEE since July 2024.
