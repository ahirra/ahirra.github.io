Interactive Research Demos

Hands-on, browser-based demos built from my published research on smart meter data analytics, smart grids and AI for energy. No installation needed.

  • Solar PV & EV Infrastructure Detector – based on A two-phase data-driven approach for detection of solar PV and EV infrastructure in smart grid, Applied Energy (2025) DOI: 10.1016/j.apenergy.2025.125912. Classifies a household's smart meter profile as normal, solar PV, EV or both, then dates the change. Open interactive demo
  • AI Data Center Load Profile Explorer – interactive analysis of 10-minute compute power demand: magnitude, variability, ramping, persistence and operating regimes, and what they mean for the grid. Open interactive demo
