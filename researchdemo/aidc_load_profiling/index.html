Daily operating regimes (K-means)

Days are clustered on 11 features (robust-scaled) and ordered from lowest to highest mean power. Change k to re-cluster; the regime profiles and grid-interaction explanations below regenerate from the data.

vs
Feature key — PR: power range · HLF / LLF: high / low-load fraction of day · LHL / LLL: longest high / low-load episode · HE / LE: number of high / low-load episodes · RUp / RDn: max ramp-up / ramp-down per 10 min. Each feature is min–max scaled (0–1) across the selected days, and bars show the regime mean, as in the notebook.
Methodology follows the accompanying notebook: ramp = ΔP between consecutive 10-min samples; load factor = mean/peak; low/high states = percentiles of the selected data; persistence = consecutive-interval runs; regimes = K-means (k-means++, 20 restarts) on robust-scaled daily features.
